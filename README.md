KEY POINTS

E-Commerce Sales Exploratory Data Analysis | Python

Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a 10,000-row e-commerce dataset with 23 columns.
Cleaned and prepared the dataset by handling duplicates, missing values, incorrect entries, and data types.
Used Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly for analysis and visualization.
Calculated key business metrics:
Total Sales: ₹23.20M
Total Profit: ₹7.30M
Total Quantity Sold: 19,143
Average Sales: ₹2,320 per transaction
Analyzed sales and profit by category, sub-category, state, city, region, customer segment, gender, payment method, and sales channel.
Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends.
Created age groups and analyzed their sales, quantity, and profit contribution.
Identified the top-performing categories and sub-categories.
Created interactive and static charts using Plotly and Seaborn.
    
📊 5 USEFULL BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. Electronics generated the highest category sales
Electronics generated approximately ₹4.66M, followed by Clothing at ₹4.31M.

2. Consumer customers contributed the highest sales
The Consumer segment generated approximately ₹13.97M in sales.

3. Maharashtra was the highest-sales state
Maharashtra generated approximately ₹4.53M in sales and 3,830 units sold.

4. Website was the largest sales channel
The Website generated approximately ₹9.06M, ahead of Mobile App, Marketplace, and Store.

5. UPI was the most frequently used payment method
UPI had 2,979 transactions, followed by Credit Card with 2,226. UPI also generated approximately ₹6.95M in sales.

📌 One more interesting insight

The 31–45 age group generated the highest sales at approximately ₹7.56M, followed by the 46–60 group at approximately ₹7.39M.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- Total Sales: ₹23.20M
- Total Profit: ₹7.30M
- Total Quantity Sold: 19,143
- Average Sales per Transaction: ₹2,320

### Category Analysis
- Electronics generated the highest sales: ₹4.66M.
- Clothing was the second-highest category: ₹4.31M.

### Customer Analysis
- Consumer customers generated the highest sales: ₹13.97M.
- The 31–45 age group generated the highest sales: ₹7.56M.

### Geographic Analysis
- Maharashtra recorded the highest sales: ₹4.53M.
- Maharashtra also recorded the highest quantity sold: 3,830 units.

### Sales Channel Analysis
- Website was the leading sales channel: ₹9.06M.
- Mobile App generated ₹7.38M in sales.

### Payment Analysis
- UPI was the most frequently used payment method with 2,979 transactions.
- UPI generated approximately ₹6.95M in sales.

### Time Analysis
- 2025 sales were ₹11.72M compared with ₹11.48M in 2024.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately ₹2.10M.
